Carnitas Tacos

Home

Description

A delicious and healthy dish that takes less than an hour to make. This can be made with or without the addition of chicken, there is plenty of protein included either way.

Ingredients

  • Carnitas pork
  • Street-size corn tortillas
  • Pickled red onion
  • Cotija cheese
  • Cilantro, chopped
  • Fresh lime wedges
  • Shredded lettuce

Carnitas

  • 4-5 lbs pork shoulder
  • 1 Tbsp kosher salt
  • 4 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 tsp chili powder
  • 2 tsp garlic powder
  • 2 tsp dried oregano
  • 2 tsp onion powder
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 4 Tbsp orange juice
  • 2 Tbsp lime juice

Steps

  1. In a small bowl, combine the salt, cumin, chili powder, garlic powder, oregano, onion powder, and black pepper.
  2. Cut the pork shoulder into several large 4-inch pieces.
  3. Cover each piece of pork with the spices.
  4. Combine orange juice and lime juice in the bottom of a slow cooker. Place pork shoulder on top.
  5. Cover and cook on low for 8 hours or on high for 4-5 hours. Turn pork throughout cooking process to ensure each side is covered by liquid.
  6. Using a slotted spoon, transfer the meat onto a large baking sheet and shred with a fork. The meat should be fall-apart tender and shred easily.
  7. To crisp, move an oven rack directly under the broiler and turn the broiler on high.
  8. Pour about 1/2 to 1 cup of the carnitas cooking liquid onto the shredded pork. Toss lightly to coat.
  9. Broil the shredded pork for 8-10 minutes, stirring halfway through, until crispy.
  10. Assemble tacos with shredded pork and other ingredients as desired. Serve.
