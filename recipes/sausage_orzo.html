Creamy Sausage Orzo

Home

Description

A delicious and healthy dish that takes about 20 minutes to make.

Ingredients

  • 1 pound of Italian sausage
  • 1/2 of a large white onion
  • 1 Tbps of minced garlic
  • 1 cup of uncooked orzo
  • 2 cups of chicken broth
  • 1 cup of heavy cream or unsweetened coconut cream
  • 2 cups of spinach
  • 1/2 cup of grated Parmesan

Steps

  1. Brown the sausage in a large pan on medium-high heat. Cook for about 5 minutes.
  2. Roughly chop the onion and add it to the pan. Cook for an additional 5 minutes.
  3. Add the orzo and garlic, cook for about 30 seconds to crisp up the orzo.
  4. Stir in the chicken broth and cream.
  5. Once it boils, cook for 10 minutes uncovered on medium-low heat. Stir often to prevent sticking.
  6. Remove pot from the heat and stir in the spinach and Parmesan until the spinach was wilted. Serve.
