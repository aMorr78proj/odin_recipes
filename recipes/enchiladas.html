Enchiladas

Home

Description

A delicious and healthy dish that takes less than an hour to make. This can be made with or without the addition of chicken, there is plenty of protein included either way.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups cooked shredded chicken
  • 2 cups enchilada sauce
  • 8 corn or flour tortillas
  • 2 1/2 cups shredded Mexican-blend cheese
  • salt and black pepper, to taste

Sauce

  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 2 Tbsp all-purpose flour
  • 4 Tbsp chili powder
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp ground cumin
  • 1/4 tsp dried oregano
  • 2 1/4 cups broth (chicken or vegetable)
  • 2 Tbsp tomato paste

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F.
  2. Measure the chili powder, onion powder, garlic powder, salt, cumin, and dried oregano for the sauce into a small mixing bowl and set aside.
  3. Heat the olive oil in a small pot over medium-high heat.
  4. Add the flour and whisk together for 1 minute.
  5. Add the spices and whisk together for 30 seconds.
  6. Slowly pour in the broth while whisking constantly to make sure no big lumps form.
  7. Add the tomato paste and whisk the enchilada sauce until completely smooth.
  8. Bring to a simmer, remove from heat.
  9. In a large bowl, combine the shredded chicken, 1/4 cup enchilada sauce, and a generous pinch of salt and pepper.
  10. Microwave the tortillas on a plate for 1 minute, flipping halfway through until all of them are warm and pliable.
  11. Assemble the enchiladas by filling each tortilla evenly with the shredded chicken mixture and 1 cup of shredded cheese. Roll the tortillas tightly to close and place in large baking dish seam side down.
  12. Pour the remaining enchilada sauce over the tortillas, top with the remaining 1 1/2 cups shredded cheese.
  13. Bake in the oven for 20 minutes until the cheese is melted and bubbly. Remove from oven and serve.
