Spinach and Artichoke Pasta

Home

Description

A delicious and healthy dish that takes less than an hour to make. This can be made with or without the addition of chicken, there is plenty of protein included either way.

Ingredients

  • 2 Tbps butter
  • 1 Tbps minced garlic
  • 1 15oz can of quartered artichoke hearts
  • 1 20oz bag of frozen, chopped spinach
  • 1 block of Boursin cheese
  • 1 cup of low fat, plain greek yogurt
  • 1.5 cups of cooked, shredded chicken
  • 8 oz of desired pasta
  • A handful of Mozzarella cheese

Steps

  1. Pre-heat the oven to 350 degrees F.
  2. Cook the pasta according to its directions until it is Al-Dente. Strain out water and set aside.
  3. Add the butter to a large pan on low-medium heat to melt.
  4. Once the butter is melted, add the garlic. Cook for 2-3 minutes, or until garlic is fragrant and golden.
  5. Roughly chop the quartered artichoke hearts and add them to the pan. Cook for 3-5 minutes
  6. Add the frozen spinach. Cook for 5 minutes on high heat to remove moisture from the spinach.
  7. Add the Boursin cheese. Stir until fully melted and mixed.
  8. Add the greek yogurt. Stir until fully melted and mixed.
  9. Remove pan from the heat. Stir in the chicken and pasta.
  10. Transfer dish to oven-safe casserole dish. Top with Mozzarella cheese as desired.
  11. Cook dish in oven for 20 minutes or until food is warmed through and Mozzarella is melted and golden-brown. Remove from oven and serve.
